Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

We Do Not Collect Personal Data

Your Privacy Is Protected by Design

Gender Predictor is designed with privacy first. Here is what you need to know:

  • No personal data collection. We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information. The dates you enter, heart rate, and any other form inputs never leave your browser.
  • No accounts. There are no user accounts on our site. We do not ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information.
  • No cookies. We do not use tracking cookies or any persistent storage beyond what you explicitly choose to save in your browser's localStorage (such as favorite baby names).

Local Processing

All Calculations Happen in Your Browser

All gender predictions are computed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. No form data is ever transmitted to our servers. The name favorites feature uses your browser's localStorage — this data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to serve the Outfit typeface. Google Fonts may log your IP address and browser information when fetching font files. For details, review Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at hello@genderpredictor.tools.

Gender Predictor is for entertainment purposes. Always consult medical professionals.
