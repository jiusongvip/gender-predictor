Signs That Point to Boy

Classic Old Wives' Tales for Boys

The History of Gender Prediction Folklore

Gender prediction through pregnancy symptoms has existed in every culture for centuries. Before ultrasound technology, expectant parents had no way to know their baby’s gender before birth. These tales filled the gap — providing comfort, conversation, and a sense of control during pregnancy.

How Accurate Is Each Sign?

What Research Actually Shows

Here’s what research actually shows about some of the most popular signs:

  • Morning sickness: Very slight correlation (girl). Not predictive for individuals.
  • Cravings: No correlation found in any study.
  • Heart rate: Zero correlation. Disproven by multiple large studies.
  • Skin changes: No correlation. Acne is driven by pregnancy hormones.
  • Hair changes: No correlation. Hair thickness changes due to estrogen.
  • Mood swings: No correlation. Mood changes from hormonal fluctuations.

Boy or Girl: What Are Your Real Odds?

Globally, about 105 boys are born for every 100 girls — a natural ratio of approximately 51% boy to 49% girl. Without any testing, your baseline odds are nearly 50/50. Any prediction method that claims more than 50% accuracy needs strong evidence to back it up.
