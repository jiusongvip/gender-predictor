The story behind the chart

Legend says the original chart was kept inside the royal palace during the Qing dynasty (1644–1911) and only left it as the dynasty fell, eventually reaching the Beijing Institute of Science. It has loose connections to the I Ching, yin and yang, and the five elements. It relies on two simple principles: the mother's age at conception and the month of conception. No single official version exists — different sites circulate slightly different grids, so if two charts disagree, that's why. One more reason to treat it as a fun tradition rather than a medical test.

How lunar age works

In the Chinese system a baby is considered 1 year old at birth, because the time in the womb counts. Everyone then turns a year older at Chinese New Year rather than on their birthday — so lunar age is usually 1–2 years more than regular age. Example: if your age at conception is 28, your lunar age is 29. The chart itself is the same every year; only how your Gregorian dates convert to lunar months shifts because Lunar New Year falls on a different date each year — in 2026 it is February 17. Our tool adjusts for this automatically.

How accurate is the Chinese gender chart?

The chart claims 50 to 90 percent accuracy depending on the source. In reality, it has about a 50 percent chance of being right, which is the same as guessing. All prediction methods on this page are for entertainment purposes only.

The Science

How is a baby's gender actually determined?

Fun predictors aside, this is what biology really says about boy or girl.

🧬

Set at the moment of conception

Every egg carries an X chromosome. Sperm carry either an X or a Y chromosome. If an X sperm wins the race, the baby is a girl (XX). If a Y sperm wins, the baby is a boy (XY). It all happens in the very first moments after fertilization.

🏁

The sperm speed myth

You may have heard that Y sperm (boy) swim faster but die sooner, and that X sperm (girl) live longer. While these are popular theories, no reliable study has shown you can meaningfully tip the odds — timing, diet, or positions included.

✅

Only two reliable answers

NIPT blood tests from around 10 weeks are 99%+ accurate. The anatomy ultrasound at 18–20 weeks is 95–99% accurate. Everything else — including every method on this page — is entertainment.
