Why Nub Theory Wins on Accuracy

Unlike the Chinese calendar which is essentially random, nub theory has genuine scientific basis. The genital tubercle (the "nub") begins differentiating into male or female anatomy between 11-14 weeks. Multiple published studies confirm 70-91% accuracy when interpreted by trained professionals — making it the most accurate non-medical gender prediction method available.

When the Chinese Calendar Still Wins

Despite its poor accuracy, the Chinese calendar has key advantages: you can use it immediately after finding out you're pregnant, with no equipment needed. It's also the only method that can be used before conception for gender planning. For pure accessibility and fun, nothing beats it.
