25+ Creative Gender Reveal Party Ideas

You have the answer (or a very fun guess). Now make it memorable. Here are reveal ideas from almost-free to showstopping, plus a budget estimator, a planning checklist and the timeline that keeps the whole thing stress-free.

Plan at a Glance

Reveal after the 18–20 week anatomy scan so it is confirmed. Pick a style that fits you — a $0 home box, a $30 cake, or a $300 event. Book any photographer 3–4 weeks out, keep one clear “reveal moment”, and only use outdoor flameless effects where legal and safe.

Virtual & Long-Distance Reveals

Family far away? Do a group video call at the reveal moment and mail identical scratch-off cards or surprise boxes ahead of time so everyone pops together on camera. A pre-taped reveal video sent at a set date works beautifully too.

Gender Reveal on a Budget

  • Coloured toilet-paper “gift” wrap — a few dollars.
  • Blue/pink grocery-store cupcakes instead of a custom cake.
  • A phone camera and tripod — skip the photographer, do a burst.
  • Balloons from a dollar store; the pop is the same either way.

Reveal Planning Checklist

  • Confirm sex via scan or NIPT; get the answer written down from one source.
  • Pick a style and a single clear reveal moment.
  • Set date (20–28 weeks is the sweet spot) and guest list.
  • Buy DIY vs book vendors early; order any custom items 3–4 weeks ahead.
  • Plan colour-safe, outdoor-only effects; check fire restrictions.
  • Assign someone to film and to keep the secret.
  • Have an “in case of wrong call” sense of humour.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should you have a gender reveal party?

After the 18–20 week scan so it’s confirmed — commonly between 20 and 28 weeks, or earlier after NIPT if you like.

How much does a gender reveal cost?

From almost nothing at home to a few hundred for an event; many of the best ideas cost under $50.

What is a low-key reveal?

A two-person cake, a letter to grandparents, or engraved jewellery — personal, no smoke or big crowd.

Are smoke bombs and cannons safe?

Use flameless, biodegradable effects outdoors only, away from dry grass, and respect local fire rules.
