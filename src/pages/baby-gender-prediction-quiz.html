Why We Still Love These Tales

Old wives' tales persist because they give grandparents, partners and friends a way to join in and place friendly bets. They also date back centuries and across nearly every culture — a shared human habit of looking for patterns. That is exactly the subject our complete guide to 15 old wives' tales unpacks sign by sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the baby gender prediction quiz accurate?

No better than chance. Each sign has been tested and none reliably predicts sex. It's a fun ritual, not a result.

How are my answers scored?

Each answer adds a point to a boy or girl column per the folk rule; the higher column wins, with a tally shown.

Do cravings really predict gender?

No. Sweets-for-girl, salty-for-boy is a myth; cravings track hormones and nutrition, not chromosomes.

Does belly shape tell you the sex?

No. High or low carrying is about your muscle tone, height and position, not the baby's sex.
