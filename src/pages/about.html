About Gender Predictor

Gender Predictor is a free, easy-to-use online tool that helps expectant parents explore the age-old question: "Is it a boy or a girl?" We combine multiple prediction methods into one result so you can see where the signs point.

What We Offer

Eight Prediction Methods in One Tool

Unlike most gender prediction sites that offer just one method, we bring together eight different approaches:

  • Chinese Gender Calendar — the ancient lunar calendar chart
  • Ramzi Theory — placenta position analysis
  • Nub Theory — genital tubercle angle
  • Heart Rate Method — beats per minute
  • Old Wives' Tales Quiz — 15 classic signs
  • Baking Soda Test — the fizzy science experiment
  • Skull Theory — ultrasound skull shape analysis
  • Ring Test — pendulum swing prediction

How It Works

Simple Three-Step Process

Enter your birth date and due date. Optionally add your baby's heart rate, baking soda test result, skull shape observation, or ring test outcome. Our tool runs each applicable method and tallies the votes. The method with more votes determines the final prediction.

You'll also get a confidence score based on how many methods agree — and which ones disagree.

Important: Gender Predictor is for entertainment purposes only. None of these methods are scientifically proven to determine your baby's gender. Only medical tests — such as NIPT blood testing (99% accurate) or the 20-week anatomy ultrasound (95-99% accurate) — can tell you for certain.
