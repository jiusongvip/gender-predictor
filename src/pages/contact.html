What We Can Help With

Questions, Corrections and Partnership Requests

  • Content corrections. If you believe a statistic, study reference, or method explanation on any page is wrong, tell us which page and what the correct information is. We review and fix verified errors.
  • Method questions. Curious how the Chinese gender chart works, what the nub theory angle means, or why the Ramzi study is not peer-reviewed? Ask away.
  • Data and privacy. For questions about how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy first — the short version is that predictions run entirely in your browser.
  • Reprints and licensing. Want to quote or reuse our charts or research write-ups? Email us — short quotations with attribution are usually fine.

What We Cannot Help With

This Is Not a Medical Service

We cannot answer personal medical questions or interpret your ultrasound results. For anything related to your pregnancy — including medically validated sex determination like NIPT blood tests or anatomy scans — please talk to your obstetrician or midwife.
