Chinese Calendar: Quick and Mathematical

The Chinese gender chart is essentially a lookup table. Find your lunar age (left column) + lunar conception month (top row), and the intersecting cell gives the result. It's fast, private, and can even be used to plan conception timing for a desired gender.

Old Wives' Tales: Social and Fun

Old wives' tales track pregnancy symptoms: morning sickness severity, cravings (sweet vs salty), belly shape (high vs low), skin changes, heart rate, and more. Each sign "votes" boy or girl.
